FAQ

What is FoodGram?

FoodGram is a food map app and food photo app where you discover real meals through real photos. You can explore restaurants and food spots on a map, save your food memories in a personal album, and share what you eat — including anonymously. It's available free on iPhone and Android.

Is FoodGram free?

Yes. FoodGram is free to use. Optional Premium (US$3/month) unlocks extra features like custom profile icons, favorite categories, ad-free experience, and a hygiene map.

Is FoodGram available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. FoodGram works on both iOS and Android. Use the App Store or Google Play links on this page to download.

Can I discover restaurants by photos?

Yes. FoodGram is built for discovering restaurants and food spots through real food photos from real people, not only ratings. Browse by photo to find meals that look delicious to you.

Can I post food photos anonymously?

Yes. When posting, you can choose to make your post anonymous. If selected, your post will be published as "Anonymous user."

Does FoodGram work for travel planning?

Yes. Use the food map to find memorable meals around the world. Discover restaurants by photos when you're planning a trip or exploring a new city.

How is FoodGram different from review apps?

FoodGram is photos-first, not reviews-first. You discover meals through real photos and "I want to eat this" instead of star ratings. It's also a personal food album and a community-built food map, not just a restaurant database.

Which languages does FoodGram support?

English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Thai, Vietnamese, and more.

Can I change my username later?

Yes. Go to Settings > Profile to update your name, username, and icon.

How can I report inappropriate content?

Use the ••• menu on a post and select Report. Our team reviews reports and may remove content or restrict accounts.

Can I block or mute users?

Yes. From a user profile, choose Block or Mute to control what you see.

Does FoodGram show ads?

Ads may be shown to free users. Premium members enjoy an ad‑free experience.

How do I cancel Premium?

Manage your subscription from the App Store or Google Play subscriptions page for your account.

What are the content guidelines?

Share food‑related, respectful, and lawful content. No harassment, spam, or illegal material.
