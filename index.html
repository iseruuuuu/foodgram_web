FAQ

What is FoodGram?

FoodGram is a food map app where you discover real meals through real photos. Explore restaurants and food spots on a map, save your food memories in a personal album, and share what you eat — including anonymously. Free on iPhone and Android.

Is FoodGram free?

Yes. The app is free to use. Optional Premium (US$3/month) adds custom icons, favorite categories, ad-free experience, and a hygiene map.

Can I discover restaurants by photos?

Yes. FoodGram is built for discovering restaurants through real food photos from real people, not only ratings.

Does FoodGram work for travel?

Yes. Use the food map to find memorable meals around the world and plan trips by real photos.

Which languages are supported?

English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Thai, Vietnamese, and more.

How do I cancel Premium?

Manage your subscription in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings for your account.
